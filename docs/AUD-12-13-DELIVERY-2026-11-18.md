## AUD-13 — Persistent Support

Support submissions now persist with a receipt, category, server-derived identity and league context. Members can return to My Requests and see their own status/history. Platform owners have a league/member/status-filtered inbox and can set New, In Progress, Resolved or Closed, with optional member-visible notes. Concurrent edits are revision-checked.

An owner in-app notice links to the support inbox. It refreshes when opening notifications and through the existing account refresh; this adds no independent polling timer or email sending.

Requests are private: verified users submit through guarded RPCs, members read only their own requests, and only platform owners manage the inbox. Idempotency, duplicate reuse, content bounds and per-user submission limits are enforced server-side. Private tables have RLS and no direct client grants.
